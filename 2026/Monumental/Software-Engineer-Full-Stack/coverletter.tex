\lettersection{Most Complex System Owned End-to-End --- Rediat}

\textbf{The problem.} Colombia's \textbf{Resolución 1888 de 2025} required every healthcare provider in the country to submit a \textbf{Resumen Digital de Atención en Salud (RDA)} --- a standardised digital summary of each clinical encounter --- as \textbf{HL7 FHIR R4}, by \textbf{15 April 2026}. The deadline was statutory. Providers had legacy systems, no FHIR experience, and a national platform that was slow, intermittently unavailable and thinly documented. We started in December 2025 with almost nothing.

\textbf{What I owned.} I chose the architecture and built it from the ground up: a \textbf{Turborepo monorepo of TypeScript/NestJS microservices} streaming send → validate → convert → generate stages over \textbf{message queues} into standards-compliant FHIR resources. Because the upstream government API failed unpredictably, I engineered \textbf{fault tolerance and resumability} into the queues so in-flight submissions recover instead of being lost, added a \textbf{Redis} caching layer so the same clinical codes are not re-validated on every request, and modelled \textbf{MongoDB} persistence for \textbf{high-throughput} multi-provider load. I built \textbf{Python/FastAPI} services for provider analytics with long-running aggregation offloaded to background workers, the \textbf{React} front end, \textbf{CI/CD on AWS}, and the \textbf{observability} (Grafana, Loki) that showed us which stage was failing.

I also owned the customer-facing half, which mattered as much as the code. I wrote the API documentation and ran long technical sessions with hospitals' health-IT leads so they could integrate and become compliant. We ended up with clearer documentation than the government's own, and I pushed the product to explain itself: actionable error suggestions, \textbf{automatic correction of the most common submission errors}, and messages in Spanish rather than English --- small things that removed a lot of friction for clinical staff.

\textbf{Outcome.} We delivered \textbf{ahead of the mandate deadline}. Roughly \textbf{10\% of all production RDAs for national health events} now flow through Rediat. We found product-market fit within a few months of starting, and the team has been growing since.
